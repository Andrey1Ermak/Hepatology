# grieve_2013_apri — full-text note

- **Source:** Grieve A, Makin E, Davenport M. Aspartate aminotransferase-to-platelet ratio index (APRi) in infants with biliary atresia: prognostic value at presentation. J Pediatr Surg 2013;48:789-795, doi:10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2012.10.010
- **Read:** 2026-11-24 from the full text (7 pages). Results pp. 790–792 (Tables 1–2, Figs. 4–5); Discussion pp. 792–794.
- **Status of document:** single-surgeon cohort, King's College Hospital London, Jan 1999–Dec 2010; data collected prospectively at laparotomy, **APRi calculated retrospectively**. No histology review — "cirrhosis" is the surgeon's **macroscopic** assessment at Kasai. No validation cohort.

## Answers to what was needed

- **What APRi > 1.22 predicts:** **macroscopic cirrhosis at the time of Kasai** (seen in 28/260, 10.8%). ROC best cut-off **1.22**: AUC **0.83** (95% CI 0.73–0.90), **sensitivity 75%, specificity 84%**, LR 4.4 in the whole cohort; in isolated BA (n = 171) AUC 0.87, sens 88%, spec 85%, LR 5.9 (p. 791, Fig. 4). It is a **severity-at-presentation** marker, not a fibrosis stage on histology. Macroscopic cirrhosis itself was linked to worse native-liver survival (P = .03, Fig. 6).
- **What APRi < 0.43 predicts:** **0.43 is the lower quartile boundary**, not an ROC-derived threshold. Only infants in the **lowest APRi quartile (< 0.43)** had significantly better **native-liver survival** than the other three quartiles (χ² 11.6, P = .009); the 2nd–4th quartiles did not separate (p. 792, Fig. 5). No sensitivity/specificity is given for 0.43.
- **When measured:** at presentation, **at the time of Kasai** (pre-operative bloods). Median age at Kasai 58 days (IQR 45–71; range 14–209) (p. 790).
- **Other cut-off: APRi > 3.0** — **no infant cleared jaundice** when APRi was > 3.0. 11 infants had APRi ≥ 3 (median age 94 days); all but one needed LT or died (3 deaths), median age 0.53 y; 4 were CMV IgM+ (p. 792). Authors suggest APRi might mark when Kasai is futile — a suggestion, not tested prospectively.
- **Varices:** APRi at Kasai did **not** predict later "significant" varices needing endoscopic therapy, and APRi at endoscopy did not either (p. 792).

## Other facts useful for cards

- Cohort: 260 infants (147 female); type 3 BA 98%; isolated 66%, anomaly-associated (incl. BASM) 18%, cystic 10%, CMV IgM+ 6% (Table 1).
- Median APRi **0.67** (IQR 0.43–1.12; range 0.11–10.81). Highest in CMV IgM+ BA (P = .007 across subgroups) (p. 791).
- APRi correlated with age at Kasai (rs 0.44), bilirubin (0.45) and spleen size (0.48) (Table 2).
- Platelets: median 536 ×10⁹/L (IQR 402–691). With the traditional > 450 limit, 68% had "thrombocytosis"; with an infant 95th centile of ~750 (Wiedmeier 2009, > 47,000 neonates; two peaks at 2–3 and 6–7 weeks), only 18% (p. 791). → infant platelet norms are higher; adult-style APRi thresholds do not transfer.
- Clearance of jaundice (bilirubin ≤ 20 µmol/L) by 6 months in 135 (51.9%); their median APRi at Kasai 0.62 vs 0.81 in non-clearers (P = .0004) (p. 792).
- Discussion (other studies, secondary citations — verify before using): Kim 2010 (35 infants, wedge biopsy at Kasai) — APRi 1.42 separated severe fibrosis/cirrhosis, AUC 0.91; in **older** children with BA APRi ≥ 1.2 predicted varices with sens 84% / spec 83% (Chongsrisawat, n = 73, mean age 9 y) and ≥ 0.96 with 86% / 81% (Colecchia, n = 31, median age 12 y) (p. 793–794).
- The APRi formula is shown only as a figure (Fig. 1); the AST upper limit of normal used is **not stated in the text**.

## Conflicts with what the app says now

- **ref_elasto / c_apri_gpr (D-2026-11-08-1: thresholds 1.22 / 0.43 from lesson 01-10 were held back pending the full text):** the numbers are **confirmed**, but their meaning must be stated exactly if published:
  - > 1.22 → predicts **macroscopic cirrhosis seen by the surgeon at Kasai** (sens 75%, spec 84%) — not "fibrosis stage", not a histologic cut-off.
  - < 0.43 → the **lowest quartile**, which had better native-liver survival; not a validated "rule-out" threshold.
  - Single center, retrospective calculation, no external validation → prognostic context only, never a verdict. BACKLOG item 1 ("add to ref_elasto as prognosis, not stage") is supported.
- If the app shows APRi with a fixed AST ULN, note that Grieve 2013 does not state which ULN was used — results may not be reproducible exactly.
