# iorio_2005 — full-text note

- **Source:** Iorio R, Sepe A, Giannattasio A, Cirillo F, Vegnente A. Hypertransaminasemia in childhood as a marker of genetic liver disorders. J Gastroenterol 2005;40:820-826, doi:10.1007/s00535-005-1635-7
- **Read:** 2026-11-24 from the full text (7 pages). Methods p. 821; results pp. 821–823, Table 1 p. 822; Fig. 1 p. 824.
- **Status of document:** retrospective single-center series, University of Naples "Federico II", consecutive referrals 1990–2003. Tertiary referral setting (authors expect a higher normalization rate in general practice, p. 823).

## Answers to what was needed

- **425 children** — confirmed: 425 consecutive children, **age 1–18 years** (208 boys), isolated hypertransaminasemia without markers of HAV/HBV/HCV (p. 821). Entry required raised aminotransferases **on at least two occasions in the month before referral**. All looked healthy; median AST 62 IU/L (48–352), median ALT 83 IU/L (65–414), normal < 40 IU/L. Found at a routine checkup in 72.3%, before minor surgery (adenoidectomy/tonsillectomy) in 27.7%.
- **Normalized:** **259 (61%)** normalized within the first 6 months **and stayed normal for the next 6 months** (p. 821). Of these: no cause 113 (43.6%), EBV 83 / CMV 42 / toxoplasma 8 (together 51.4%), drug-induced 13 (5%; isoniazid 6, aspirin 3, amoxicillin-clavulanate 2, valerian 2).
- **166 persistent (> 6 months)** — confirmed (39%). Causes (pp. 821–823): obesity-related liver disease **75 (45.1%)**; **genetic 51 (30.7%)**; cryptogenic 22 (13.2%); gallstones 5 + choledochal cyst 3 (8, 5%); autoimmune hepatitis 7 (4.2%; 1 type 1, 6 type 2); celiac 3 (1.8%).
- **Genetic disorders (Table 1, n = 51):** **Wilson 18**, **muscular dystrophy 14**, alpha-1 antitrypsin deficiency (PiZZ) 4, Alagille 4, hereditary fructose intolerance 4, glycogen storage disease IX 3, OTC deficiency 2, Shwachman syndrome 2 — all **confirmed**. Genetic disease = 12% of the whole cohort (51/425) and 30.7% of persistent cases.

## Other facts useful for cards

- alt_elevated (Wilson): all 18 asymptomatic, median age 7.5 y; KF ring in only 1 (age 14); **all had ceruloplasmin < 20 mg/dL**; basal urine copper > 100 µg/24 h in only 10/18; diagnosis confirmed by liver copper in all (median 800 µg/g dry weight) (p. 822).
- alt_elevated (muscle): CK checked in **all** patients; muscular dystrophy suspected from CK; AST (median 123) > ALT (median 80); no muscle signs noticed before referral; median age 2.2 y (pp. 822, Table 1).
- alt_elevated (A1AT): suspected from absent alpha-1 peak on serum protein electrophoresis; mild GGT rise, normal bilirubin (p. 822).
- alt_elevated (Alagille): 4 girls, no jaundice or pruritus, normal bilirubin, GGT 2.5–4.5× normal, murmur in all; paucity on biopsy in all (p. 822–823).
- alt_elevated: raised GGT with normal bilirubin led to gallstones or choledochal cyst on ultrasound (8 children, none presented with jaundice or pain) — authors call GGT a reliable cholestasis marker here (pp. 821, 823).
- alt_elevated (HFI): aversion to fructose foods, hepatomegaly, severe steatosis on US, no hypoglycemia history (p. 823).
- alt_elevated (AIH): 6 of 7 were type 2 (anti-LKM1); none had acute onset (p. 821).
- Cryptogenic 22: biopsy in 18 showed minimal–moderate chronic hepatitis only; biopsy gave no etiologic information (pp. 823, 825).
- No difference in aminotransferase level between etiologic groups (p. 823) — the height of ALT did not point to the cause.

## Conflicts with what the app says now

- **alt_elevated (Why) / cases.json:** all numbers match the full text. Two precision points:
  - "One in three" persistent cases genetic — exact figure 30.7% (51/166).
  - The cohort **already had two raised values within a month** before referral. "Repeat first; the second abnormal result is where treatable diseases hide" is a fair reading, but the 61% normalization figure is from children who had already been repeated once — it supports watchful repeat over 6 months, not a single recheck.
- Age range: the cohort is **1–18 years** — the Naples numbers should not be applied to infants under 1 year (alt_elevated has infant/neo branches).
