### 03-13 · Biliary atresia: the adult with BA (synced 2026-11-05)
BA is a model for malignancy in a cholestatic, cirrhotic native liver; every number — TODO(verify) against the cited paper at build.
- **Systematic review (slide 15; Madadi-Sanjani O et al. Pediatr Surg Int 2025;41:165, 37 publications):** 57 malignancies in BA — 42 HCC, 8 cholangiocarcinoma (CCA), 5 hepatoblastoma, 2 other (bowel, thyroid); reported cases, not incidence; publication bias certain.
- **HCC (slides 15–16):** median age 4 y (7 months–34 y; a 38-year-old also reported); persistent jaundice in 77% of those with data; AFP raised in 68% with data → **a normal AFP does not exclude HCC** (and infant AFP norms differ — use `labs.json` bands); often incidental in the explant; 28 alive without recurrence after LT. King's (Hadžić N et al. J Pediatr 2011;159:617–22): 3/387 infants (0.8%, 1990–2008), median age 2 y, + 2 referred cases found in explants; only 2/5 raised AFP; all 5 transplanted and well.
- **CCA (slides 15–16):** median age 23.5 y (11–63); long-standing Roux loop, chronic biliary injury, stones, residual cysts; CA 19-9 raised in all 3 with data (CEA also mentioned); presents symptomatically — new stricture, porta mass, cholangitis that will not settle; 4 of 5 with outcome data died (Nio M et al. Surg Case Rep 2019;5:185: 63-year-old, cyst-type BA, unresectable hilar CCA). → Don't miss (adult/teen with BA): "A new hilar stricture is a tumor until proven otherwise — MRI/MRCP, CA 19-9, tissue, transplant team."
- **Surveillance (slide 17) — no BA-specific guideline; label "Adult cirrhosis guidance, extrapolated" and "Expert proposal":** every native-liver survivor — US + AFP every 6 months (AASLD 2023, Singal AG et al. Hepatology 2023;78:1922–65; EASL HCC CPG J Hepatol 2018;69:182–236: US every 6 months, AFP optional); persistent jaundice in childhood — US + AFP every 3–6 months and yearly CA 19-9 from adolescence (Madadi-Sanjani 2025 proposal, not a guideline); poor US window (adhesions, small liver) → contrast MRI; LT candidate → cross-sectional imaging at listing.
- **Guideline compare (slide 18):** HCC surveillance — DIFFERS (AASLD US + AFP vs EASL AFP optional); CCA surveillance in BA — lesson MATERIAL, no document addresses it → GAP by §5.2, MATERIAL reading in `note` (03-06/03-07 rule; DECISIONS at build).
- **Pitfall (slide 20):** stopping surveillance after childhood — HCC reported into the fourth decade.

### 03-14 · Block 3 synthesis: biliary atresia (synced 2026-11-06)
- **Synthesis (pearl 20, slides 3, 14):** native-liver tumours in BA split by age — HCC in young children (median 4 y), cholangiocarcinoma in adults (median 23.5 y) (57-tumour review — Madadi-Sanjani O et al. Pediatr Surg Int 2025;41:165, as in the 03-13 entry; Hadžić 2011 is the King's series); 57 tumours published, no surveillance guideline (gap). Surveillance frame: US + AFP every 6 months for life, CA 19-9 in adults; any new biliary stricture in an adult native liver = cholangiocarcinoma until proven otherwise.
