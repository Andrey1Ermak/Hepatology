### 03-05 · Biliary atresia: the Kasai operation (synced 2026-10-27)
- **How common (slides 2, 7, 20):** cholangitis after the Kasai in 30–60%; ~57% of 223 US infants had ≥ 1 episode in the first year (Kelley-Quon 2022, Western Pediatric Surgery Research Consortium, Pediatr Surg Int 2022;38:193–9, online 2021). Cholangitis is the most common complication.
- **Why (slide 7, for Why):** bacterial access to a poorly draining, fibrotic biliary tree — not simple reflux of bowel content; antireflux valves, stomas and long conduits did not reduce cholangitis. Gallbladder Kasai keeps the sphincter of Oddi (rationale, not proven benefit).
- **Fever + new jaundice after clearance (slides 19, 21):** cholangitis is the first diagnosis and is treated first — most recurrences are infection, not anastomotic; a redo Kasai is rarely the answer (only after good initial flow that stopped abruptly). → First contact: blood cultures + IV antibiotics; low threshold (high-volume pathway, slide 15).
