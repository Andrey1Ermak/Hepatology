### 05-04 · PFIC IV: MDR3 deficiency (ABCB4) (synced 2026-11-24)
- **LPAC syndrome (low-phospholipid-associated cholelithiasis; Rosmorduc 2003, Dong 2021 JHEP Rep 3:100201 — VERIFIED full text PMC7848766):** diagnosis needs 2 of 3 — symptoms before 40, recurrence after cholecystectomy, intrahepatic hyperechoic foci or comet-tail images. LPAC vs controls: intrahepatic lithiasis 90.6% vs 0%; recurrence after cholecystectomy 86.4% vs 13.3%; 77% women, median age 27 at first symptoms; ABCB4 variant in 46%; about 1% of symptomatic gallstones (0.5–1.9%). **Pediatric hook:** 12% had first symptoms before 18 years (youngest 8).
- **Management (EASL 2024; TODO(verify)):** lifelong UDCA (adult dose 8–10, up to 13–15 mg/kg — do not move to a pediatric card without a pediatric source); cholecystectomy does not cure; endoscopic clearance of duct stones; hepatectomy only for localized macrolithiasis. Teen with recurrent biliary pain after cholecystectomy → think LPAC, image the intrahepatic ducts, offer ABCB4 testing to patient and family.
- **Pitfall:** repeated surgery for recurrent pain — LPAC is intrahepatic.
