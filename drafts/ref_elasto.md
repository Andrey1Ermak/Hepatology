### 03-09 · Biliary atresia: portal hypertension after the Kasai (synced 2026-10-31)
- **Adult Baveno VII rule does not transfer (slides 10, 17, 19):** "LSM < 20 kPa + platelets > 150 → skip screening endoscopy" (de Franchis R et al. J Hepatol 2022;76:959–74) and CSPH "LSM ≥ 25 kPa" are not validated in children; small BA cohorts suggest high negative predictive value only in older children. → Pitfall line on the card.
- **Varices in biliary atresia (lesson cites Chongsrisawat V et al. BMC Gastroenterol 2011;11:41 — TODO(verify)):** liver stiffness 12.7 kPa, AUC 0.89 (sens 84%, spec 77%); APRI 1.92, AUC 0.87. These cut-offs predict varices of any size, not high-risk varices — the lesson: NITs are "poor at excluding high-risk varices". Single cohort — show as an association, not a threshold to act on. (→ c_apri_gpr, built with this card on day 23 — read this entry then.)
- **Spleen stiffness:** MR elastography 9.9 kPa, AUC 0.84, n = 22 (Yoon H et al. WJG 2019;25:367–77 — TODO(verify)) — "promising, small".
- **Post-Kasai use (slide 18, expert framework):** yearly elastography ± spleen stiffness where available; rising stiffness → repeat endoscopy. Cross-link: 03-08 entry in chol_0_3m — LSM > 19.9 kPa at 3 mo predicted liver-related events (Hahn 2013). Not the same as the Baveno "< 20 kPa" rule (adult, skip-endoscopy) — unrelated cut-offs that happen to be close; never merge them.
