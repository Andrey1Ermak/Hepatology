### 02-14 · Block 2 synthesis (synced 2026-10-22)
- Evidence for the bands: native-liver survival at 4 years 49% with Kasai ≤ 30 d, 36% at 31–90 d, 23% > 90 d (Canada, n = 349, Schreiber 2007); French national cohort — survival falls with every 15-day band (Serinet 2009).
- Targets differ (slide 5, MATERIAL): AAP 2025 — before 30 days; Fawaz 2017 — as early as possible, no number; UK median ~50 days. Lesson slide 2 says 30–45 days; case 1 "before day 45 at the latest". Show 30 / 45 / 60 as bands with the source of each; record wording in DECISIONS.
- Breastfed infant first fractionated at 4 weeks has already used most of a 30-day target — show "days left to 30/45/60" prominently.
- Preterm: use postnatal age for the clock (Kasai in preterm atresia came at 72 vs 53 days, largely because cholestasis was blamed on PN — slide 14). TODO(verify) whether any source specifies chronological vs corrected age; if none, say "Limited data".

### 03-04 · Biliary atresia: timing of the Kasai (synced 2026-10-26)
- **Present as a gradient, not a deadline (slides 2–3, 18):** bands ≤ 30 / 31–45 / 46–60 / 61–90 / > 90 d; at every band the message is "operate as soon as possible"; past 60 or 90 days the calculator must **not** say "window closed" — show two lines: "No ascites, INR corrects with vitamin K, no cirrhosis → Kasai now" / "Ascites, INR not corrected by vitamin K, or cirrhosis → transplant assessment first", plus "Discuss with a transplant center" (slide 18).
- Band evidence: France native liver at 25 y 38% / 27% / 22% / 19% by month 1 / 2 / 3 / > 3 (Fanna 2019); Serinet 2009 — operating all before 46 d would spare ~5.7% of transplants (evidence shown under the 31–45 d band, slide 2); Hoshino 2023 ≤ 30 vs 31–60 d RR 1.13 (the first month helps even inside the usual target).
- Targets by source (slide 17): AAP 2025 — before 30 days (2-week visit advised); European centers — "first 4 weeks" (02-14 entry said "Europe: no number" — reconcile); Fawaz 2017 — no number per 02-14 (TODO(verify) full text).
