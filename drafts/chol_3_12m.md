### 02-11 · Syndromic cholestasis (synced 2026-10-19)
- The exam (face, heart, vertebrae, eyes per §4.2) extends to joints, skin, kidneys, glucose: they point to a karyotype, sterol profile or genome rather than another liver panel.
- Other syndromic causes: Alagille (JAG1, NOTCH2 — commonest syndromic cholestasis, block 4); tricho-hepato-enteric (TTC37, SKIV2L: intractable diarrhea, woolly hair, immunodeficiency); NISCH (CLDN1: ichthyosis, scalp hypotrichosis, sclerosing cholangitis); Zellweger spectrum (VLCFA).
- Attenuated ARC survives beyond infancy (19 survivors aged 3–59 y, 18 with VPS33B variants — Zhu & Chen 2022; milder disease with missense variants, one causing isolated low-GGT cholestasis — Qiu 2019). Rule stays absolute: suspect ARC → no percutaneous biopsy; if a procedure is unavoidable, plan it with hematology.
- Negative cholestasis panel in a dysmorphic infant → trio genome with copy-number analysis; reanalyze every 2–3 years (EASL 2024: at least every 3 years).

### 02-12 · Genetics in the infant (synced 2026-10-20)
- Leading genes vary by origin (slide 5): US panels — JAG1, SERPINA1, ABCC2, ABCB11, CFTR (Hoskins 2026); China — SLC25A13 (citrin) commonest; Japan/Korea — neonatal Dubin–Johnson (ABCC2) and citrin; Türkiye — recessive low-GGT genes (consanguinity). Pick a panel that matches the local spectrum **and reports copy-number changes**; otherwise trio exome/genome.
- In Shanghai all 17 genetic disorders could present at 0–3 months; only 5 also presented after 4 months.
- Post-Kasai infant with poor bile flow and JAG1 on the panel → re-label as Alagille; IBAT inhibitor eligibility; family testing (slide 19).
- Normal report is not "not genetic" when the phenotype moves on: reanalyze (EASL 2024: at least every 3 years); VUS → phase with parents, re-analyze (3117 VUS vs 662 P/LP in 2171 tests, Karpen 2021).

### 02-13 · Supportive care in the cholestatic infant (synced 2026-10-21)
- Same supportive program at any age (Mouzaki 2019): energy 130%, protein 130–150%, fat 30–50% with MCT:LCT 30:70 (TODO(verify) MCT share in Mouzaki 2019); length + MUAC + triceps skinfold every 2–4 weeks — weight is inflated by organomegaly and ascites.
- Vitamins every 3–6 months (monthly if severely cholestatic): retinol:RBP, 25-OH D, vitamin E:total lipid ratio, INR ± PIVKA-II; red-cell fatty acid profile every 3–6 months; urea/creatinine every 3 months (→ c_vitamins). A normal INR does not prove adequate vitamin K for bone.
- **UDCA in ABCB4 (MDR3, high-GGT PFIC):** the one setting with a real target — ~87% of patients with ≥ 1 missense variant responded, none with severe genotypes (Gonzales 2023, n = 38); EASL 2024 recommends it (strong). Guideline compare candidate (slide 18): UDCA in ABCB4 — North America nothing specific vs EASL recommended with ≥ 1 missense variant (strong) — DIFFERS. Outside ABCB4: not first line (EASL), no controlled data in Alagille. EASL: insufficient data for or against UDCA in AATD.
- **Itch ladder (slide 14, EASL 2024 ratings):** skin care + night-time sedating antihistamine for sleep only (marginal) → rifampin ~5 mg/kg/day, range 4–10, check liver tests (moderate; complete relief 15%, partial 36%, Gregorio 1993) → sertraline 1–4 mg/kg/day (14/20 improved, Thébaut 2017) → IBAT inhibitor (Alagille, selected PFIC) → diversion (responsive BSEP genotypes) or transplant. Cholestyramine binds vitamins and UDCA — separate by hours; marginal. TODO(verify) rifampin/sertraline doses, maximum per day and frequency against EASL 2024 before publishing.
- **Alagille itch:** EASL recommends offering an IBAT inhibitor (strong). ICONIC (maralixibat) and ASSERT (odevixibat) positive for itch and bile acids. Class effect: diarrhea → watch fat-soluble vitamins. Labels (lesson, 2026): FDA maralixibat Alagille ≥ 3 mo, odevixibat Alagille ≥ 12 mo; EMA odevixibat ≥ 6 mo — TODO(verify) current labels at card build.
- Measure itch at each visit (caregiver scratch score or ItchRO(Obs)); sleep loss is the outcome families feel.
- HPS: oximetry upright and supine routinely in chronic cholestasis; < 97% → investigate (changes transplant timing).
- Case candidates (slide 21): Alagille, 14 months, scratching all night despite rifampin → IBAT inhibitor, watch diarrhea and vitamins; biliary atresia, 9 months, SpO2 95% supine → investigate for HPS.

### 02-14 · Block 2 synthesis (synced 2026-10-22)
- NICCD (pearl 12): high GGT, very high AFP and citrulline; lactose-free MCT formula; almost all recover by 12 months. Biallelic SLC25A13 → no surgery **only if it explains the phenotype**; persistent acholic stools → cholangiogram anyway (02-12).
- Alagille + Kasai: transplant 47% vs 14%, death 32% vs 3% (Kaye 2010) — late-presenting infant with suspected atresia: echo, spine film, eye exam before the cholangiogram.
- Weight z-score −2.1 vs −1.2 at 6 months after Kasai separated poor from good outcome (DeRusso 2007) — measure the arm, not only the weight.
