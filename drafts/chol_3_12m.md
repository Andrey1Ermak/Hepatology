### 02-11 · Syndromic cholestasis (synced 2026-10-19)
- The exam (face, heart, vertebrae, eyes per §4.2) extends to joints, skin, kidneys, glucose: they point to a karyotype, sterol profile or genome rather than another liver panel.
- Other syndromic causes: Alagille (JAG1, NOTCH2 — commonest syndromic cholestasis, block 4); tricho-hepato-enteric (TTC37, SKIV2L: intractable diarrhea, woolly hair, immunodeficiency); NISCH (CLDN1: ichthyosis, scalp hypotrichosis, sclerosing cholangitis); Zellweger spectrum (VLCFA).
- Attenuated ARC survives beyond infancy (19 survivors aged 3–59 y, 18 with VPS33B variants — Zhu & Chen 2022; milder disease with missense variants, one causing isolated low-GGT cholestasis — Qiu 2019). Rule stays absolute: suspect ARC → no percutaneous biopsy; if a procedure is unavoidable, plan it with hematology.
- Negative cholestasis panel in a dysmorphic infant → trio genome with copy-number analysis; reanalyze every 2–3 years (EASL 2024: at least every 3 years).
