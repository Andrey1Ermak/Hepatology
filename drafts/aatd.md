### 03-03 · Biliary atresia: diagnosis (synced 2026-10-25)
- **A1AT mimics atresia on biomarkers:** high GGT, and MMP-7 above the 52.8 ng/mL single-plex cut-off in 8 of 17 A1AT-deficient infants in ChiLDReN (Pandurangi 2024). Biopsy can show ductular changes that mimic atresia (slide 14).
- **A1AT level and phenotype at presentation**, in parallel with GGT, MMP-7 and the ultrasound (slides 15–16) — a pending result never delays the cholangiogram.
- Case (slide 20): high MMP-7, PiZZ phenotype, pigmented stools → A1AT deficiency; avoid laparotomy.
