### 02-14 · Block 2 synthesis (synced 2026-10-22)
- **When to fractionate (slides 2, 4, 19):** formula-fed and jaundiced at 2 weeks; breastfed, jaundiced and well by 3 weeks (Fawaz 2017) / at 3–4 weeks (Kemper 2022); **pale stool or dark urine at any age → total + direct bilirubin the same day**. Breastfed and thriving at 3 weeks → total and direct whatever the stool color (self-test, slide 18).
- **Look up the newborn direct bilirubin** of every jaundiced infant: a value above the lab range is a signal, not a footnote (Harpavat 2025 AAP; Harpavat 2011 — atresia is abnormal from birth). Day 2 direct 0.5 with lab limit 0.3 → abnormal: repeat by 2 weeks, ask about stools.
- **Threshold:** direct/conjugated > 1.0 mg/dL is abnormal at any total (Fawaz 2017); the 20% rule is dropped — 79% of atresia had direct/total ≤ 0.2 on day 2 (Harpavat 2011). Card text must not use a ratio rule.
- Breast milk and thriving explain nothing once direct > 1.0 mg/dL ("breast-milk jaundice" is an unconjugated diagnosis).
- **Stool color:** ask at every visit 2–4 weeks; look at the stool yourself; teach parents the card. A normal card is not reassurance in a jaundiced infant (pigmented stools early in atresia); a pale stool is a same-day referral (slide 7).
- Stool card sensitivity depends on the program: Taiwan national 2005 97.1% (Hsiao 2008), meta-analysis 79.6% (7 studies, 996,262 infants, Gu 2020 — TODO(verify) source of the pooled figure), Tochigi 76.5%, British Columbia passive distribution 50% (3/6, Woolfson 2018); PPV 4–29% — Why/Pearls material, not Duty.
