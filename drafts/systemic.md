### 04-12 · Alagille syndrome XII: other causes of ductopenia (synced 2026-11-18)
- **Hodgkin lymphoma and vanishing bile duct syndrome (Ballonoff 2008, Acta Oncol 2008;47:962–70; slide 16):** 37 published cases of Hodgkin-related VBDS or idiopathic cholestasis; 1-year overall survival 43%, liver-failure-free 41%; 12 of 20 with complete response liver-failure-free; better with stage I–II, complete response, radiotherapy. Cholestasis can precede the diagnosis; ducts recover only if the lymphoma is controlled. → Teen with unexplained ductopenia/cholestasis + fever, weight loss or nodes → image the chest. Verified against the abstract (PMID 17906981) 2026-11-18.
- **Langerhans cell histiocytosis** attacks ducts and can leave sclerosing cholangitis needing transplant (lesson, no number).
- **Trisomy 21:** neonatal cholestasis 3.9% in a population cohort, always with heart, gut or marrow involvement; severe cases had marrow disease (Arnell 2012, verified) — see chol_3_12m Inbox.
