### 04-12 · Alagille syndrome XII: other causes of ductopenia (synced 2026-11-18)
- **Bile duct loss is the ominous sign (DILIN, Bonkovsky 2017, Hepatology 2017;65:1267–77; slide 10):** 363 biopsied cases over 10 years, duct loss in 26 (7%); chronic injury 94% with duct loss vs 47% without; 7 of 26 died or were transplanted despite steroids and ursodiol; degree of duct loss the strongest predictor. Agents: amoxicillin-clavulanate, temozolomide, herbal products, azithromycin, others. Mostly adults. Verified against the abstract (PMID 27981596) 2026-11-18.
- **Drug-induced vanishing bile duct syndrome in children (slide 11):** fever, rash, sometimes SJS, then cholestasis that outlasts the drug. Stop the drug; document all exposures incl. supplements; image to exclude obstruction; persisting cholestasis for months → biopsy with CK7 rather than wait on ursodiol. Ursodiol and steroids used without trial evidence (AASLD 2023 DILI guidance, EASL 2019 — TODO(verify) wording). RUCAM scores causality but says nothing about ducts.
- **Guideline compare (slide 17):** treatment of drug-induced VBDS — GAP.
- **Pitfall:** six months on ursodiol after a drug reaction instead of biopsying persisting cholestasis.
